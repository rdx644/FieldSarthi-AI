## Suggested narration
Field fault reports often get lost between a voice note and a supervisor's to-do list. FieldSaarthi turns a worker's short report into a clear next step. Gnani Prisma transcribes the actual audio. We match that text against known inventory and require the worker to review it. Confirmation creates one durable order, with retry protection. A supervisor can assign and resolve it, while the original transcript and each change remain inspectable. Gnani Timbre can speak a saved-ticket acknowledgment, but saving never depends on speech playback. Our demonstration uses synthetic data, selected-language short clips and human triage.
