# ADR-008: Synthetic data and temporary audio

Status: Accepted implementation decision, recorded retrospectively on 2026-11-09 from merged code and the roadmap; remaining gaps are not approved away.

## Context
The demo needs useful evidence without real customer/employee records or public voice archives.

## Decision
Seed synthetic users/assets/locations, keep consented clips and raw responses private, delete application decode temporaries.

## Alternatives considered
Long-lived public raw audio or customer data is unnecessary for the submission.

## Consequences
Private operator test assets have a separate consent/retention lifecycle. Review final video and provider retention; do not claim regulatory compliance.
